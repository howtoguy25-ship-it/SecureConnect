┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│           APP FEATURE & SECURITY TESTING PROMPT             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Perform a comprehensive QA test on the app and verify the
following features work as intended. For each item, report:
✅ Pass, ❌ Fail (with details), or ⚠️ Partial (with notes).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. CHAT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Send/receive text, media, voice notes, and files
• Confirm end-to-end encryption is active (protocol, key
 exchange, indicators)
• Test delivery, read receipts, typing indicators, offline
 queueing
• Verify forward secrecy and that messages can't be read
 server-side

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. STATUS / STORIES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Post text, photo, and video statuses
• Confirm 24-hour expiry and auto-deletion
• Test privacy controls (who can view, mute, reply)
• Verify encryption in transit and at rest

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. LOCKER
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Confirm files/media are encrypted at rest (state algorithm,
 e.g., AES-256)
• Test PIN/biometric lock, failed-attempt handling, recovery
• Verify contents inaccessible outside the app (file system,
 backups, screenshots)

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. CALLS (AUDIO & VIDEO)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Place/receive 1:1 and group calls
• Confirm BOTH audio and video are end-to-end encrypted
 (SRTP/DTLS or equivalent — state which)
• Test quality, reconnection, mute, camera switch, screen share
• Verify no media is stored or routed unencrypted through
 servers

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. LOGOUT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Confirm logout clears session tokens, cached data,
 notifications
• Verify user must re-authenticate fully to return
• Check no residual data remains on device

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. DELETE ACCOUNT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Confirm full deletion: profile, chats, status, locker,
 contacts, media, backups
• Verify deletion propagates server-side (not just local)
• Confirm username/number can't reach a "ghost" account
• Test grace period (if any) and final irreversible state

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. VIRTUAL PHONE NUMBERS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Generate, assign, and verify a virtual number
• Test inbound/outbound calls and SMS on the virtual number
• Confirm the user's real number is never exposed
• Test number recycling, deletion, and porting (if supported)

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DELIVERABLE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A pass/fail report per feature, with screenshots or logs for
any failures, plus a security summary specifically confirming
encryption status for chat, locker, calls (audio + video),
and status.
