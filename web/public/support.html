Common issues

Location isn't showing / the map is stuck on a default spot

Check that location permission is allowed for TrackLine in your browser or phone's app settings, and that location services are turned on for the device generally.

AI Vehicle Detection won't start, or the camera view is blank

Make sure camera permission is allowed for TrackLine. On first use your browser/phone will prompt for it — if you denied it, you'll need to re-enable it from your device's permission settings.

I didn't receive the SMS verification code

Double-check the phone number, wait a minute in case of delivery delay, and make sure the number can receive SMS (some VoIP/landline numbers can't). If it still doesn't arrive, email us the number you're trying to verify and roughly when you requested it.

I want my account or reported alerts deleted

Email us and we'll remove your account data directly — there isn't a self-service delete button in the app yet.

An alert on the map looks wrong or stale

Alerts are reported by other users and expire automatically (45 minutes to 24 hours depending on type). If one still looks wrong, you can tap it to hide it from your own view, or report it again if it's genuinely resolved.

Traffic light / speed camera markers seem off

Those come from OpenStreetMap's community map data, not an official government feed — occasionally missing or out of date in less-mapped areas. You're welcome to fix it directly at openstreetmap.org, which TrackLine will then pick up automatically.
